## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Last alternatives: Die Together**. This trainer adds configurable gameplay assistance for exploring cursed islands, gathering treasure, avoiding monsters, and returning safely to the ship. It is designed for single-player use and controlled private sessions, with options focused on survival, movement, interaction range, and loot collection.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more relaxed way to explore cursed islands, experiment with the elastic-arm physics, collect treasure, and learn the game's environments without constant pressure from monsters or extraction risks.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Activate movement assistance before leaving the ship and adjust the speed gradually. Moderate values are best for navigating tight interiors, caverns, ships, and castle areas without losing control. Disable the option when precise physics interactions or cooperative positioning are required.

### Tools & Inventory

Use the trainer alongside the normal treasure-collection loop. Pick up valuable objects with the elastic arms, organize the crew's carried booty, and return it to the ship before ending the expedition. Treasure protection is most useful during the trip back, but it does not replace careful route planning or teamwork.

### World & Environment

Expeditions take place across chapters with distinct biomes, environmental effects, hazards, and monsters. Use extended interaction range to examine distant objects, dismantle doors, move furniture, and recover loot from awkward positions. Stealth assistance is recommended when learning patrol routes or exploring unfamiliar areas.

### Combat & Weapons

The game emphasizes survival and evasion rather than conventional combat. Use monster damage protection when testing enemy behavior or exploring dangerous areas. Keep the crew together, communicate when a monster is nearby, and prioritize bringing the collected treasure back to the ship.
